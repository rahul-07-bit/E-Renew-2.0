    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is E-waste?
    E-waste refers to discarded electronic devices such as old computers, smartphones, tablets, and televisions.
    What is the proper way to dispose E-waste?
    Take your e-waste to certified recycling centers or collection points to ensure safe and responsible disposal.
    How much time is taken to recycle E-waste?
    Recycling time depends on the item type and facility but typically takes a few weeks for full processing.
    Which precious metals can be extracted from E-waste?
    E-waste contains valuable metals like gold, silver, copper, and palladium that can be recovered.
    How much wealth can we generate from E-waste approximately?
    Globally, e-waste recycling generates billions of dollars in recovered valuable materials each year.
